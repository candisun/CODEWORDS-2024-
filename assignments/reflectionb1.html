01 Research deeply, prototype freely

My approach to starting any project typically begins with research, and for the initial stage of my paper prototype, I focused on exploring the theme of cybernostalgia. While I was passionate about the concept, its vastness left me unsure of how to navigate it. To gain clarity, I turned to reading, which has always been a valuable resource. By diving into articles, theses, books, and statistical data, I was able to ground my concept with solid information.

This research deepened my understanding, allowing me to design more thoughtfully and creatively based on a well-defined framework. Once I had a stronger grasp of cybernostalgia, I shifted my attention to creative coding examples, seeking out visuals and interactions that resonated with me. This phase was particularly enjoyable as it opened up a wealth of inspiration, sparking my interest in creative coding and introducing me to several projects that aligned with my aesthetic and conceptual goals.

When it came to creating the paper prototype, I appreciated the freedom it offered. I could be as imaginative and "unrealistic" as I wanted, without the constraints of coding, which I often find challenging. This allowed me to explore all my ideas freely and focus on the core concept, leaving technical concerns for later stages. Reflecting on this process, I recognise areas for improvement. For future projects, I would avoid getting caught up in details like typography and colour too early, as these decisions can be made during the refinement stage. Focusing too soon on these aspects may limit my ability to explore alternative ideas and outcomes.


Cybernostalgia: A Longing for the Pre-Digital World

Cybernostalgia refers to the emotional longing for the tactile, personal experiences of the pre-digital era. This concept highlights how the digital age contrasts with the more grounded, physical realities of the past.

Digital Amplification: The digital era offers numerous ways to engage with nostalgic elements, such as retro gaming and social media throwbacks. These digital interactions allow individuals to relive cherished memories, fostering deeper emotional connections and a sense of identity.

Creative Reflection: Cybernostalgia is not just about looking back, but also about reflecting on how past experiences influence our understanding of the future.

[Source: Embracing the New Nostalgia in Today’s Digital World](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/embracing-the-new-nostalgia-in-today-s-digital-world-0850421c8f49)


The Positive Role of Nostalgia

Research reveals that nostalgia, often bittersweet, primarily serves as a positive force. It enhances emotional well-being, self-continuity, and helps overcome personal challenges.

Psychological Constructiveness: Scholars demonstrate that nostalgia is not merely melancholic but serves as a constructive tool, especially in overcoming present crises.

Futuristic Visions: In the context of AI and cybernetics, nostalgia enables creative thinking, offering both utopian and dystopian visions of the future.

[Source: Constructive and Creative Role of Nostalgia](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352250X22002421?via=ihub#section-cited-by)


Anticipated Nostalgia: Projecting Future Longing

Anticipated nostalgia refers to the expectation of feeling nostalgic for present experiences in the future. This concept bridges the past, present, and future self, playing a crucial role in emotional regulation.

Triggers: The most prominent triggers of anticipated nostalgia are interpersonal relationships and meaningful interactions with loved ones.

Study Findings: A study found that 90% of participants could identify events they expected to feel nostalgic about in the future.

[Source: Study on Anticipated Nostalgia](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352250X23000064)


Mental Transportation and Self-Continuity

Nostalgia facilitates "mental transportation," allowing individuals to mentally leave their current space and immerse themselves in autobiographical narratives.

Higher Self-Continuity: This mental transportation helps build a sense of connection among one's past, present, and future selves, strengthening global self-continuity.

Narrative Space: When mentally transported, individuals construct meaningful life stories that connect their life transitions, linking their past, present, and future selves.

[Source: Mental Transportation and Global Self-Continuity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352250X22002421)

03 Selling no Apple juice!!

Doc-45456
Through this project, I gained a deeper understanding of the importance of subtlety in design. Being too literal can often restrict the user’s ability to fully engage with the concept, as it leaves little room for personal interpretation. By intentionally leaving space for ambiguity, I found that users can interact with the design on their terms, allowing their imagination to shape their experience.

This idea was reinforced by Andy’s comment: “You’re not asked to sell apple juice,” reminding me that my work isn’t about delivering a product with a fixed message, but rather creating a concept that invites exploration. This shift in thinking emphasised the value of balancing clarity with openness. The challenge lies in crafting a structure that guides the user without dictating their experience, creating room for individual reflection and creativity.
