fish n chips!

During a trip to the beach, I encountered a fish and chips shop that, at first glance, seemed to be a cosmetic or beauty care establishment. The typography, color scheme, and logo design all conveyed a message inconsistent with what one typically associates with a fish
and chips place. This initial misperception left me hesitant to enter the shop, even though it
was nearby.

This experience highlighted the importance of effective visual communication in design. The choice of typeface, color, and shape plays a critical role in conveying the intended message. In this case, the shop's design elements failed to communicate its purpose clearly, leading to confusion and a reluctance to engage.

Reflecting on this, I am reminded of the necessity for designers to ensure that their visual elements align with the message they wish to convey. This incident has reinforced my commitment to thoughtful design, emphasising clarity and coherence in visual communication.
