"Creativity is not a talent; it is a way of operating."

play with puzzles

John Cleese's "Creativity in Management" speech, delivered to Video Arts in 1991, became iconic for its exploration of creativity in everyday contexts, particularly in the workplace. Video Arts, a British training company co-founded by Cleese, specialised in humorous training videos. Using humor and insight, Cleese offered practical advice on fostering creativity, breaking from conventional productivity methods, and embracing "open mode" thinking. His insights promote creative freedom, showing that creativity flourishes as a playful process rather than a structured skill.

One of Cleese’s most impactful lines is: "Creativity is not a talent; it is a way of operating." This concept inspired me to approach creative coding as more than just functional code but as a fluid, imaginative process. The idea of being in a “state of play” allows me to see coding problems as playful puzzles instead of strict tasks. This openness encourages me to combine unexpected ideas, experiment freely, and embrace curiosity, making each project a blend of creativity and functionality.
