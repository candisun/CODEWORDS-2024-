words become pictures?

I found reading "META/DATA: A Digital Poetics" by Mark Amerika particularly engaging, especially when I transformed one of its phrases into a concrete poem. The text lends itself exceptionally well to this format, allowing the abstract ideas to become more expressive and dynamic. In the form of concrete poetry, the text goes beyond mere words—it becomes a visual composition that communicates energy and movement.

Quoting Andy, "Words are just little pictures". In constructing a concrete poem, the arrangement of these "little pictures" forms a visual image that complements the meaning, creating a synergy between content and design. This connection between the metadata text and the concrete poem allows for a more layered interpretation, where the structure of words and the deliberate use of space are as important as their meaning, transforming the poem into a multidimensional experience that extends beyond traditional text.
