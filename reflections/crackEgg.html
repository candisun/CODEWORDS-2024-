I set ambitious goals during my Hello World exercise, hoping to create an animation effect. However, with my limited knowledge of HTML and CSS, I turned to two YouTube tutorials in Visual Studio Code for guidance. The process was time-consuming, as I needed to research almost every line of code to grasp its meaning fully. While I adapted the tutorial's code, I realised that my limited understanding made it difficult to apply it flexibly. This experience highlighted

an important lesson: while tutorials can help achieve specific results, it's essential to understand the underlying logic to use code creatively and effectively. Moving forward, I plan to build on the foundational structures I’ve learned rather than attempting to implement advanced concepts prematurely. This approach will allow me to grow steadily while still exploring new ideas. It is like learning to crack an egg before baking a cake.

During the Crazy Eight activity, I initially felt uneasy as it pushed me out of my comfort zone. However, I quickly recognised the value of working under a time constraint. It prevented me from over-refining a single idea and encouraged me to generate a wider range of concepts.

As I progressed, I noticed my ideas evolving, becoming more inventive with each iteration. This exercise highlighted the importance of rapid ideation, and I plan to incorporate this method into my design practice, particularly during the ideation stage, to encourage more creative exploration.
